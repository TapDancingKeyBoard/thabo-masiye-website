Introducing MentorLink

Turning Business Potential into Sustainable Growth

Discover MentorLink →

At MentorLink, we believe that every enterprise has the potential to grow, compete and create meaningful economic impact when equipped with the right strategies, capabilities and opportunities.

We are a business advisory and consulting firm committed to bridging the gap between business ambition and execution. Our expertise lies in identifying growth barriers, strengthening organisational capabilities, enhancing market competitiveness and preparing enterprises to access new opportunities.

Through evidence-informed business diagnostics, tailored strategic advisory, supplier development, innovation and capacity building, we translate business challenges into practical, measurable and sustainable solutions.

We work with entrepreneurs, established businesses, corporations and development partners to build resilient enterprises, strengthen value chains and advance inclusive economic growth.

At MentorLink, our commitment goes beyond providing advice. We focus on enabling transformation, unlocking opportunities and creating lasting value.

Meet the Founder

Thabo Joy Masiye

Founder & Lead Strategist · MSME Growth & Innovation Strategist

Thabo Joy Masiye is an economist, Accredited Supplier Development Practitioner and Certified Business Mentor with over eight years of experience in enterprise development, innovation and inclusive economic growth.

Her work focuses on transforming enterprises through business diagnostics, growth strategy, operational excellence, supplier development and market competitiveness. She has supported entrepreneurs and enterprises across Southern and East Africa, helping businesses strengthen their capabilities, overcome growth barriers and access new opportunities.

A 2017 Mandela Washington Fellow trained in human-centred design at Dartmouth College, USA, she combines global perspectives with practical business expertise.

Through MentorLink, she is committed to building competitive enterprises, unlocking opportunities and driving sustainable economic transformation.
