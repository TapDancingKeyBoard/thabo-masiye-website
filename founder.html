Founder's Professional Profile

Thabo Joy Masiye

Founder & Lead Strategist · MSME Growth & Innovation Strategist

Professional Biography

Thabo Joy Masiye is an economist, Accredited Supplier Development Practitioner and Certified Business Mentor specialising in enterprise development, business transformation, innovation and market competitiveness.

With a professional background spanning financial audit, strategic business advisory, entrepreneurship development and international development, she brings a multidisciplinary approach to strengthening enterprises and unlocking sustainable growth.

Her expertise encompasses business diagnostic assessments, growth strategy, operational optimisation, supplier readiness, market access and enterprise development programme design. She works with entrepreneurs, businesses and institutions to translate strategic ambitions into practical business solutions.

Her professional journey spans corporate institutions, government, international development organisations and entrepreneurship ecosystems, giving her a comprehensive understanding of the challenges and opportunities facing African enterprises.

A 2017 Mandela Washington Fellow with training in human-centred design at Dartmouth College, USA, Thabo combines international perspectives with a deep understanding of African business environments.

As the Founder and Lead Strategist of MentorLink, she is committed to building competitive enterprises, strengthening business ecosystems and advancing inclusive economic transformation across Africa.
