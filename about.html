About Us · Who We Are

Building Enterprises. Shaping Economic Futures.

MentorLink is a Zambia-based business advisory and consulting firm dedicated to strengthening enterprise competitiveness, driving innovation and enabling sustainable economic growth.

Who We Are

We provide tailored, evidence-informed solutions that help businesses navigate complex challenges, strengthen operational capabilities and capitalise on emerging market opportunities.

Working with entrepreneurs, corporations, development institutions and ecosystem partners, we combine strategic insight with practical expertise to deliver solutions that create lasting economic and business value.

Our ambition is to contribute to the development of a thriving, competitive and inclusive African enterprise ecosystem.

Our Story

Bridging the Gap Between Potential and Performance

MentorLink was founded on the belief that business potential alone is not enough. Enterprises need the right strategies, capabilities, systems and networks to compete, grow and remain resilient.

Inspired by practical experience across corporate environments, international development programmes and entrepreneurship ecosystems, MentorLink was established to bridge the gap between business advice and effective implementation.

Our purpose is to equip enterprises with the tools, knowledge and strategic direction needed to turn challenges into opportunities and ambitions into sustainable results.

Our Vision

To be a leading African business advisory and consulting firm, recognised for transforming enterprises, advancing innovation and driving sustainable economic growth.

Our Mission

To empower enterprises and organisations through strategic advisory, innovative solutions, capacity development and meaningful partnerships that enhance competitiveness, unlock opportunities and deliver sustainable growth.

Our Consulting Philosophy

Strategy Meets Execution

We believe effective business advisory goes beyond identifying challenges and providing recommendations. It requires a deep understanding of each enterprise, its operating environment and its growth ambitions.

Our consulting approach is diagnostic-led, collaborative, tailored and action-oriented. We work alongside our clients to develop practical solutions, strengthen implementation capabilities and establish clear pathways towards sustainable growth.
